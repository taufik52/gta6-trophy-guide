    Roadmap to the platinum

    Five stages, in order, built from the official trophy descriptions. The stage of each trophy is shown on its card in the checklist.

      Missable trophies

      Fourteen trophies need a moment that can pass you by. Do them when the guide says to — most belong to the story and partner moments, so clear them before the final mission.

        About this guide

        This guide is an offline web app. It loads only its own files, saves your checklist in this browser with localStorage, and never sends anything anywhere. Export downloads your progress as a file; import reads that file back.

        How to use it

        1. Follow the roadmap stages in order, and tick each trophy off as it pops.
        2. Turn the spoiler setting on if you have not finished the story: it hides the names and descriptions of hidden story trophies until you earn them.
        3. Use the per-trophy counters for the counted trophies (stunts, races, collectibles) so you always know how far is left.
        4. Write a note on any trophy — a location, a loadout, a reminder — and it is saved with your progress.

        Sources

        Every trophy's name, description and grade is the official one, as listed by Exophase and PSNProfiles. Each trophy's guide cites those two lists beside it, and the guide text itself is written for this guide from the official description. Where the official description does not pin down a place or a step, the guide says so instead of inventing one.
