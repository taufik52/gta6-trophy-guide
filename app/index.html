Safe by design

Chapter trophies (Chapters 1–4 and the final mission) and every named-mission trophy unlock as you play the story and cannot be skipped past — you only need to finish the mission. Activities, collectibles, exploration and economy trophies are all repeatable in free roam after the story.

How the tracker works

Tick a trophy and it stays ticked — progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) and never leaves your machine. Export downloads a small JSON file; import reads one back. The spoiler switch hides story trophies (name, description and guide) until you earn them. Nothing on this page makes a network request: it works offline from its own files.
